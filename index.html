Team split

Teams

A + B registers 60 members (40 main, 20 subs). A only registers 30.

No-show penalty

Members marked No-show last time go to the back of the line for one event.

  1. Members who have waited the most events without a main slot go first.
  2. When the wait is equal, last time's subs go first, then higher 1st team power.
  3. The first 40 in line play main, the next 20 are subs, the rest sit out. Power then decides Team A or Team B.
